Telemetry The service's analysis beside the app's

Each flight recorded by the new telemetry pipeline, analysed on the service, next to the same flight as the app uploaded it. Differences beyond what the parity check allows are marked. Landing scores are expected to differ on firm landings: the service sees the impact, the app's once-a-second sample usually misses it. Under each flight, the Discord post the service would make from its own analysis, beside the app's. The service's is not posted. Then what the flight page, the feed and the aircraft stats would show from each; a figure only the service has, such as fuel used, which the app never sends, is not marked.
